How MindNotes works:

A triangle chip would be attached to the side of your head. This chip detects when a thought is running through your mind. It will then prompt you on the watch (included with purchase), "Would you like to transfer your thought to notes app?". If yes, then click the button on the right side of the watch. If no, then click the button on the left. The thought will be translated and put on the notes app on your phone if you allowed the program to do so.
